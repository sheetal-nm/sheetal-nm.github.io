A multi-distributor platform that enables offline retailers to run branded online stores on unique subdomains.

The ecosystem connects platform administrators, distributors, retailers, retailer teams and customers, while FYND Commerce acts as the system of record for core commerce data and order fulfilment.

01

PLATFORM CONTEXT

One platform. Multiple business roles. Shared commerce infrastructure.

Navtatva is designed around a multi-distributor model where retailers can operate branded storefronts while still working inside a broader commerce ecosystem.

SYSTEM OF RECORD

FYND Commerce is used for core commerce data and order fulfilment, while Navtatva layers its multi-role and retailer-facing experience on top.
